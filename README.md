# ISTARY: The Isochrones and Stellar Tracks for Astrophysics Research from the YREC collaboration
From Istari: 
  The Wizards or Istari in J. R. R. Tolkien's fiction were powerful angelic beings, Maiar, who took the physical form and some of the limitations of Men to intervene in the affairs of     Middle-earth in the Third Age, after catastrophically violent direct interventions by the Valar, and indeed by the one god Eru Ilúvatar, in the earlier ages.

All Isochrones were generated using the ```YREC_isochrone_guide_final.ipynb``` file in ```yrec_user_tools``` repository. 

Isochrones cover the ages 250Myr, 500Myr, 750Myr, and from 1.0Gyr to 14.0Gyr. Isochrones for M67 and the Pleiades are also included.
