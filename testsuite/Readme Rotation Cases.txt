We provide 9 sample cases for models that explore different treatments of rotation and angular momentum loss. Five of these are for an intermediate 2 solar mass star (m2p0 feh+0.0 *); four are for a lower MS star (m0p7 feh+0p0*) in the test suite.

m2p0 feh+0.0 vzams150 IES1 TAMS has rotation initialized on the ZAMS, solid body, with the release paper default mixing (weak mu gradient suppression).
m2p0 feh+0.0 vzams150 IES2 TAMS has rotation initialized on the ZAMS, solid body, with the original YREC mixing, similar to MESA (strong mu gradient suppression).
m2p0 feh+0.0 vzams150 DRIES2 TAMS has rotation initialized on the ZAMS, differential rotation, with the original YREC mixing, similar to MESA (strong mu gradient suppression).
m2p0 feh+0.0 vzams150 NoJMixIES2 TAMS has rotation initialized on the ZAMS, differential rotation, no angular momentum transport, original YREC mixing, similar to MESA (strong mu gradient suppression).
m2p0 feh+0.0 vzams150 NoJNoMixIES2 TAMS has rotation initialized on the ZAMS, differential rotation, no mixing and local angular momentum conservation.
m0p8 feh+0.0 rotp8p0d0p1mag1e4std_TAMS Fast launch case (8 day period, 1e5 yr disk locking), core envelope decoupling from Somers&Pinsonneault 2016, standard wind model.
m0p8 feh+0.0 rotp8p0d0p1mag1e4cus_TAMS Fast launch case (8 day period, 1e5 yr disk locking), core envelope decoupling from Somers&Pinsonneault 2016, custom wind model.
m0p8 feh+0.0 rotp8p0d0p1mag1e4nowind_TAMS Fast launch case (8 day period, 1e5 yr disk locking), core envelope decoupling from Somers&Pinsonneault 2016, no wind.
m0p8 feh+0.0 rotp8p0d0p1mag1e4norossby_TAMS Fast launch case (8 day period, 1e5 yr disk locking), core envelope decoupling from Somers&Pinsonneault 2016, no Rossby scaling for the wind.
